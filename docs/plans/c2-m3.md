# Plan: c2-m3

- Started: 2026-10-12
- Cycle: C2, milestone M3, exchange and the accept (`docs/ROADMAP.md` §C2,
  ADR-0027)
- Idea: verbatim from the human: "C2 M3"

## Goal

An assembly leaves ArriX and comes back through STEP with its structure, in
metres. `arrix import` and the app's `Import…` read a STEP file's product
tree into one part per product (its solids as `core.import-step` features in
the product's own frame), one assembly per assembly product, and components
grounded at the file's placements, a product placed twice being one part
used twice. `arrix export --format step` and the app's `Export…` write an
assembly's tree back with `write_products_with` in metres: each part once,
each component an occurrence, nested assemblies as sub-assemblies, and
appearance colours. A document holds materials (a density as an
expression, an appearance colour), assigned per part with a per-body
override, and the evaluator gives mass, centroid and inertia per body, part
and assembly, on the eval line and the protocol. C2's accept passes: a
bracket, servo and horn assembly with one vendor part round-trips, and its
reading by ArriX matches Open CASCADE's reading, frozen in a committed text
golden written by a script that never runs in CI.

## Non-goals

- Measure, section view, 3MF, DXF/SVG, interference (the named cycle "the
  mechanical core completed").
- Mass properties and materials in `arrix-plugin-api` (but for the
  `Density` kind, ADR-0033): the API's
  `MassProperties` stays volume, area and centroid. C3's robotics plugin
  asks for them through an API change when it needs them.
- A material library or presets shipped with the app (Open question 3).
- Writing a STEP in millimetres (ADR-0028 §5), IGES, healing a vendor file
  (A7), sheet bodies from Arris 0.8's surface-model reader (refused at
  import as `kernel.import.sheet`).
- Joints in STEP: the file carries placements only, so an imported
  assembly is grounded components, and an exported one writes its solved
  poses.
- Textures, per-face appearance written back beyond what the reader's
  `FaceColour` carries in, transparency.

## Design deltas

- **ADR-0031, materials and mass properties** (step 2):
  `Document.materials: BTreeMap<MaterialId, Material>`; `Material { id,
  name, density: Expr (density kind), appearance: Rgb }`; `Part.material:
  Option<MaterialId>` and `Part.body_materials: BTreeMap<SlotRef,
  MaterialId>` for a final body that differs. Mass of a body is density ×
  Arris's unit-density properties; a part sums its final bodies; an
  assembly sums its components at their solved world poses (parallel-axis
  theorem), pure math in `arrix-doc`. A body without a material is
  `mass.no-material` (volume kept, mass not summed, the part and assembly
  totals flagged partial): fail soft, never a silent default density.
- **ADR-0032, STEP with product structure** (step 2, after the probe):
  the import's shape (one part per product, a feature per solid, its
  frame; instances share a part; names `import.<feature>.<entity>`, instance
  0), what a colour becomes (a material per distinct colour, density
  unset), the export's mapping (a part is a product written once, a
  component an occurrence at its solved pose relative to its parent
  assembly, final bodies its solids, material appearance its colour), and
  what is lost each way (joints, component names: Open question 5).
- **`arrix-core`**: `QuantityKind::Density`, units `kg/m3` and `g/cm3`;
  it is the plugin API's enum too, so plugin API 0.6.0 (breaking), ADR-0033
  (found at step 3).
- **`arrix-doc`**: the material model and commands (`AddMaterial`,
  `EditMaterial`, `DeleteMaterial` refused while assigned as
  `material.in-use`, `AssignMaterial { part, body: Option<SlotRef>,
  material: Option<MaterialId> }`, each with its inverse); `MassOutcome`
  per body, part and assembly in the evaluation; `import_products(doc,
  file, &StepStructure) -> Command` building the import's one `Group`;
  `core.import-step` type version 2 with a `frame: world | product`
  parameter (a v1 record migrates to `world`, its current behaviour).
- **Schema 5**: `materials/<id>.json` and the part's two new fields
  (omitted when empty). 4 → 5 is the identity but for the header; fixture
  `tests/docs/migrations/schema-4/`, a copy of `tests/docs/assembly/` as
  saved at schema 4, re-saved to its bytes.
- **`arrix-kernel`**: `MassProperties` gains `inertia` (about the centroid,
  unit density); `read_step_structure(bytes)` returns a plain
  `StepStructure` (products, occurrences, placements, names, colours, solid
  indices), recorded; `import_step_in` takes the frame (`import_step` is the world one); `export_step` writes
  metres through `write_with` and `export_step_products(bodies, tree)`
  through `write_products_with`, both recorded, `kernel.export.unit`
  retired.
- **Surfaces** (ADR-0026, additive): the eval line gains `materials` and
  `mass` (per body, part, assembly); the protocol gains the material
  commands and `mass` in its evaluated events; `eval --schema` and `serve
  --schema` refreshed. `arrix import <step> -o <dir>` is a new CLI verb.
  `arrix-plugin-api` at 0.6.0 for the `Density` kind alone. Found at
  step 8: the app may not read the file's structure on its thread, so the
  protocol also gains `Request::ReadStepTree`, `Outcome::Reading` and
  `SessionEvent::StepTree` (a plain `StepTree`, additive), and the
  kernel's `StepStructure` is converted to it by `arrix_doc::read_step_tree`;
  `Import…` is then two frames, the request and the one command.
- **App**: `Import…` reads the tree; the viewport colours a body by its
  material's appearance; a material section in the part's property panel
  (pick, create, edit).

## Arris dependencies

- `arris_io::step::read` with `ReadOptions { length_unit: Metre }` and
  `Read::products: ProductTree` (`Occurrence`, `Rgb`, `FaceColour`; Arris
  ADR-0033): in the pinned `arris` 0.6.
- `arris_ops::measure::mass_properties` with `inertia` about the centroid:
  in 0.6.
- `arris_io::step::write_products` (product tree, colours): in 0.6, but
  millimetres only.
- **A12**, `step::write_with` and `write_products_with` with
  `WriteOptions { length_unit }`: landed on Arris's `main` (its plan
  `step-unit-and-sheet-bodies` step 1), **unreleased**. Steps 9 to 12 wait
  on the release that carries it (0.8, which also breaks `step::read`:
  a surface model reads as a `Sheet`).
- A product's body in its own frame: the reader returns each solid at its
  world placement only. Step 1 establishes whether the inverse placement
  through `ops::transform` is good enough; if not, it is a new ask (A13,
  "read each product's solids once, in the product's frame") and step 7
  waits on it.

## Acceptance

`cargo test -p arrix-cli --test c2_accept`: the accept document exported
with structure in metres, read back by ArriX, and held to the committed Open
CASCADE golden of the same file and of the vendor file; with it, the whole
gate green (`.githooks/pre-commit`, `.githooks/pre-push`) and `cargo test
-p arrix-app --test visual` passing `import_structure`.

  step 1's numbers; human decides in ADR-0032 at step 2. Step 1 found the

- OPEN 2 (answered, ADR-0031 §2: per part with a per-body override): materials per part with a per-body override (proposed), or per
  body only. Human, by step 2.
- OPEN 3 (answered, ADR-0031 §6: document-only): a material library (steel, aluminium, ABS, PLA) as a user
  config file (ADR-0015), or document-only materials in M3 with presets a
  backlog line (proposed). Human, by step 2.
- ⚠ OPEN 4: the vendor part: a NIST CTC/FTC file from Arris's real corpus
  (public domain, read cleanly there) copied into the accept document's
  blobs (proposed), or a manufacturer's servo file kept out of the repo and
  fetched by the script. Human, by step 11.
- ⚠ OPEN 5: component names: Arris's `Occurrence` carries the product's
  name only, so a component's own name (`servo`, `left screw`) does not
  survive the round trip. Accept the loss, or ask Arris to read and write
  the `NEXT_ASSEMBLY_USAGE_OCCURRENCE` name. Human, by step 10.
- ⚠ OPEN 6: if Arris 0.8 is not released when step 8 lands, the plan
  waits at step 9; asking Arris for a release is the human's. Human, at
  step 9.
